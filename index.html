01 / about this place

A home for the in-between.

I wanted somewhere less formal than a profile and more alive than a changelog. So this repository became a home for web experiments, small prototypes, unfinished thoughts, and things that are simply fun to build.

Some things here may be polished. Others may still be wearing their debugging labels. That is not a bug in the concept; it is the concept.

Personal does not have to mean human. Sometimes it just means: this space has a point of view.
